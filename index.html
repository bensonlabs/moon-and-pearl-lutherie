The atelier

A few enduring priorities guide every instrument.

The work is shaped by honest materials, refined proportion, and an instrument that feels alive in the hands.

01

Material honesty

Chosen woods and components are selected for character, integrity, and longevity.

02

Voice first

Response, balance, and resonance lead the process long before any decorative decision.

03

Quiet finish

The surface should support the instrument, not overpower it. Restraint creates presence.

Archive & restoration

New builds sit beside older instruments brought back to life.

Preservation work is approached with respect for original intent, historical detail, and the life already present in the piece.

Consult

Listen closely to the instrument, its player, and the practical needs of the project.

Assess

Document structure, finish, playability, and any issues that affect long-term stability.

Repair

Use the least intrusive intervention that solves the problem while preserving character.

Voice

Set up the instrument so it feels balanced, responsive, and ready for musical use.
