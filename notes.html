01 / Technique
Binding

Straightening binding with a tape roll

I reverse-coiled binding inside a roll of tape to help straighten it. The roll held the binding while I worked against the curl it already had.

This was one of the simpler fixtures I’ve used in the shop.

“Reverse coiling binding inside a roll tape to straighten it”

02 / Useful scraps
Fixtures

Making jigs from acrylic scraps

I use acrylic offcuts for router bases, templates, and jigs. I’ve also shared a router sled base, a side-dot jig, and a modified fret-dressing stick on Instagram.

These projects help me hold a part or guide a tool for a particular operation.

“Acrylic scraps are usually free and great for making router bases, templates, and jigs.”

03 / Trial & adjustment
Experiments

Is it dumb if it works?

“Is it dumb if it works?” was the title of one of my workshop posts. I share experiments there, including the attempts that need another try.

Some involve a small change, like adding a notch to a fret-dressing stick or adjusting how I hold a piece.

“Conclusion: not terrible.”
