A little color, inlaid

Custom dot inlays bring small points of turquoise color to wooden fretboards.

The smallest details can carry the most personality.

Seen together, the dots establish a rhythm along the neck. Seen individually, their color and variation give each little circle a character of its own.

The archive photograph brings the fretboards and source beads into the same frame. It is a useful view of the relationship between a small material and the finished detail.
